Drag a bob to pose it; release to continue. Pose edits become its new reset state. Drag an anchor diamond to move it (all anchors move together in shared mode). Mechanisms must be visible for dragging. Pause to arrange several before releasing. Lengths are relative units; angles are measured from downward vertical.
